The Maison

The first Champagne in the world to be encased & finished in 24K Gold.

Our finest Champagnes include very rare vintage years from 1996 & 1998, the only vintage Champagnes in the world finished in real 24K Gold, 18K Rose Gold & Palladium. Each casing is patented and uniquely handcrafted in England, and it does more than dazzle: the precious metal shields the wine from light and from changes in temperature.

About · Enjoy Life

A name of love, victory & honour

Héloïse

After the 12th-century French writer and scholar, celebrated for her intellect and her love letters, the patron of passion.

+

Lloris

From the Latin for laurel, the wreath placed on the brow of victors and the emblem of honour since antiquity.

I

Rare 1996 & 1998

Rare vintage collections, laid down in the chalk cellars of Champagne and released only by the bottle.

II

Traditional

Disgorged on demand. Traditional winemaking, so each vintage reaches the glass at its freshest.

III

Since the 14th century

Craftsmanship dating back to the 14th century, from the Premier Cru slopes of Chigny-les-Roses.

Tasting note

Héloïse Lloris Vintage Brut 1996

Crafted from a blend of Chardonnay and Pinot Noir sourced from vineyards in Épernay. The vintage benefits from a warm growing season, emphasising ripe fruit, while extended ageing on the lees brings a nuanced palate.
